# Radio Vainer

Live audio from this computer, heard in the browser.

The site is static. The studio tab mixes your microphone with music playing on the machine and sends that mix straight to listeners. The picture from screen share is never sent.

**Listen:** https://yanivvi.github.io/radio-vainer/

**Studio:** https://yanivvi.github.io/radio-vainer/studio.html

## Go on air

Use Chrome or Edge on the computer that has the microphone and the music.

1. Open the studio.
2. Turn on the microphone.
3. Capture music. Choose **Entire Screen** and turn on **Share system audio**. If the music is a browser tab, share that tab and its audio instead.
4. Go live.
5. Leave the studio tab open and the computer awake.

Listeners open the radio page and press Listen. A small audience is the comfortable range, because each person connects directly to the studio tab.
